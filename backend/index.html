Works best on clean, simple songs with clear chords. Chords are detected as major or minor triads. Transposing changes the chart, not the audio. With a capo set, chords are shown as the shapes you play. Songs you analyze are saved on this device so they reopen instantly; the audio is sent to the server to be analyzed, and the resulting chords are shared by audio fingerprint so nobody has to analyze the same file twice. For a YouTube link, the official player plays the video while your browser listens to this tab and builds the chart live — nothing is downloaded, and only the chord timeline is saved.
